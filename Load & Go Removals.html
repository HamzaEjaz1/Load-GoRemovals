FAQ

Questions people ask.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. If the rubbish is outside or easy to reach, we can collect while you’re out and send you a photo when it’s done.

Do you do the lifting?

Yes. That’s the whole point. We carry everything from wherever it is, including stairs and backyards.

How is the price worked out?

By volume: how much of the tray your rubbish takes up. Labour and tip fees are included in the quote you receive.

Is it cheaper than a skip bin?

For most small and medium jobs, yes. You also skip the waiting, the permits and the loading yourself.

What happens to my rubbish?

We separate metals, cardboard, green waste and usable items first. Only what can’t be recycled or donated goes to landfill.

Can you do regular pick-ups for my site?

Yes. Builders and businesses can book weekly or fortnightly runs.
